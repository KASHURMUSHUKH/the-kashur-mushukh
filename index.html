OUR STORY

A scent with
a sense of place.

The Kashur Mushukh is imagined as a tribute to Kashmir — its quiet mornings, saffron fields, flowering gardens, snow-covered peaks and timeless hospitality.

We blend a luminous opening with a floral heart and a deep, woody dry-down to create a fragrance that feels both familiar and unforgettable.
