THE ESSENCE OF KASHMIR

More than a perfume.
A memory of home.

Kashmir has always been more than a place. It is a feeling — the silence of snow-covered mountains, the fragrance of flowers carried by a valley breeze, and memories that remain long after we leave.

The Kashur Mushukh was born from that feeling. A fragrance inspired by the landscapes, traditions and timeless beauty of Kashmir.

OUR STORY

From the valley,
with a story to tell.

Every fragrance carries a story. Ours begins in Kashmir.

A land of mountains, gardens, forests, flowers and memories. A place where a particular breeze, a particular season or a familiar scent can take you back in an instant.

The Kashur Mushukh is our interpretation of that feeling — contemporary in character, yet deeply connected to the soul of Kashmir.

We believe a fragrance should do more than simply smell beautiful. It should make you remember.
