THE ESSENCE

Kashmir, in a bottle.

There are places that you visit, and there are places that stay with you.

The Kashur Mushukh was created from the latter — the fragrance of Kashmir's gardens, its mountains, its mornings and the memories that remain long after you leave.

Elegant, intimate and distinctly Kashmiri, every fragrance is designed to become part of your own story.

OUR STORY

Born from memory.

Kashmir has a fragrance of its own. The freshness of the mountains, blooming gardens, warm spices, wooden homes and the quiet beauty of the valley create memories that are difficult to describe.

The Kashur Mushukh is our attempt to capture that feeling. Not simply a perfume, but a small reminder of Kashmir that travels with you.
